                                warning
                                Anticipated Learner Difficulties & Pedagogical Solutions
                            

                            

                                

                                    
                                        

                                            Anticipated Linguistic / Classroom Difficulty
                                            Teacher Intervention & Solution (CCQ / Drill)
                                        

                                    
                                    
                                        

                                            1. Students might confuse "bandwidth" (technical internet capacity) with human cognitive/work capacity.
                                            Use CCQ: "If I say I don't have the bandwidth for this project, am I talking about my computer or my schedule/energy?" (Schedule/energy).
                                        

                                        

                                            2. Pronunciation stumbling block on "synergy" with hard 'g' instead of soft /dʒ/.
                                            Choral drill: Model /ˈsɪnədʒi/, isolate the soft /dʒ/ like gem or magic, mark syllable stress on the first syllable.
                                        

                                        

                                            3. Students slipping back into native L1 during free group negotiation stage.
                                            Provide structured sentence frames on whiteboard and assign a student "Language Chairperson" to keep groups in English.

                            Board Plan
                        


                        
                        

                            

                                
                                

                                    

                                        Left: Vocabulary & IPA
                                        (Lexical Focus)
                                    

                                    

                                        
• leverage /ˈlɛv.ər.ɪdʒ/ (v)
= use to maximum advantage

                                        
• synergy /ˈsɪn.ə.dʒi/ (n)
= combined working power (1+1=3)

                                        
• bandwidth /ˈbænd.wɪdθ/ (n)
= capacity/time to handle work

                                        
• touch base /tʌtʃ beɪs/ (idiom)
= briefly talk / reconnect

                                    

                                


                                
                                

                                    

                                        Center: Grammar & Concept Timelines
                                        (Form & Meaning)
                                    

                                    

                                        

                                            
Negotiation Framing:

                                            
"Could we [verb]..." (Polite proposal)

                                            
"Were we to [verb]..., we would..." (Formal)

                                        

                                        

                                            
CCQ Core Question:

                                            
"Are we talking about computers or human capacity?" ➔ Human capacity!

                                        

                                    

                                


                                
                                

                                    

                                        Right: Instructions & Homework
                                        (Action Items)
                                    

                                    

                                        

                                            
Pair Negotiation Task (15 min):

                                            
1. Form pairs (Marketing vs. Dev)

                                            
2. Agree on budget using at least 3 target idioms

                                            
3. Present 1-min summary to class

                                        

                                        
Homework: Complete Worksheet Section 4 essay prompt by next session.
